# Textures

All from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com), CC0 (public domain): free to
use for anything, no credit required; credited here anyway. Re-encoded as
1K JPEGs; `_albedo` is the colour, `_normal` the normal map (OpenGL),
`_arm` ambient occlusion, roughness and metal (as glTF reads them).

## Facades

`facade_*` are made by `tool/textures/facades.py`: a photographed wall,
resampled to its real size and tiled, with windows drawn into it (glass,
frames, mullions, sills, recesses), 8 × 8 bays of 1.7 × 2.6 m.
