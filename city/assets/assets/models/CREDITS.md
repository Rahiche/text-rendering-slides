# Models

Made in Blender by `tool/blender/*.py` (trees, tram, vehicles, forge,
people) unless listed here.

## Scans

From [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com), CC0 (public domain): free to use
for anything, no credit required; credited here anyway. Brought down to the
city's budget by `tool/blender/props.py` (a node per material, standing on
the origin at real size); their textures resized to 512 px by
`tool/blender/prop_textures.py`.
